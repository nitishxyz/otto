Apply targeted, multi-hunk, or multi-file patches. Read every target immediately before patching; copy context and indentation exactly. Wrap input in `*** Begin Patch` / `*** End Patch` and batch related edits in one call.

Preferred exact-replacement form:
```text
*** Begin Patch
*** Replace in: path/to/file.ts
*** Find:
old text
*** With:
new text
*** End Patch
```

Standard add/update/delete form:
```text
*** Begin Patch
*** Add File: path/to/new.ts
+new content
*** Update File: path/to/existing.ts
 context line
-old line
+new line
 context line
*** Delete File: path/to/old.ts
*** End Patch
```

Line-number directives are available after reading the file:
```text
*** Begin Patch
*** Replace Lines in: path/to/file.ts
*** Lines: 20-30
*** With:
replacement
*** End Patch
```
Use `Delete Lines`, `Insert Before`, or `Insert After` similarly; ranges are 1-indexed and may end with `end`/`eof`/`$`.

Rules:
- Context/removal lines must match current file text verbatim, including tabs and spaces.
- Include enough unique context around updates; use 3+ lines for YAML.
- Use one file section with multiple hunks for multiple changes to that file.
- `*** Find:`/`*** With:` pairs belong under `*** Replace in:`, not `*** Update File:`.
- In `*** Update File:` hunks, prefix every content line with ' ', '+', or '-'.
- `fuzzyMatch` permits whitespace-normalized matching; disable it for strict matching.
- `allowRejects` applies valid operations while returning rejected ones.
- On failure, reread the file, then either rebuild the hunk from current content or switch to `*** Replace Lines in:` with fresh line numbers.
